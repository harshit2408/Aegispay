# P1-16 — AP and finance research

Status: pending. On October 27 the owner confirmed that the three real conversations have not yet happened. No interviews, participant quotes or findings are claimed.

Use three separate 15-minute conversations with people who handle AP, finance approvals or goods receiving. Capture each person's role and workflow without names, supplier secrets or real invoice/account identifiers.

1. Ask them to describe their last invoice discrepancy: which records they trusted, who checked the receipt and how long resolution took.
2. Ask what stops an invoice payment, how a changed supplier account is verified, and who may override a hold.
3. Show the explicitly mocked Evidence, Security and Money panes. Ask them to locate the accepted quantity, locked payee, reason for a hold and next eligible action without coaching.
4. Ask which evidence is missing, which wording is unclear and whether they would approve a partial payment in this workflow.
5. Finish with the one change that would make the workflow useful. Ask permission before quoting their feedback anonymously.

For each conversation record: date, role, interview method, actual problem and frequency, observed task result, consented quote, requested change, and the resulting product decision. Distinguish a participant's statement from our interpretation. Add findings only after the conversations occur; leave unanswered questions blank.
