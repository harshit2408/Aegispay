Adopt TokenOps Tier 1 through the shared app.agents.invoice_run boundary described in docs/TOKENOPS.md. A2/A3/A4/A5 must share one run and inject its governed completion function; direct provider calls are forbidden. The boundary uses a $1 invoice allowance and four provider requests including retries/continuations, trusted inclusive input bounds, enforced output limits and sticky refusal after uncertain usage. A1 authoring and A6 simulator workflows need separately bounded executions. Preserve R2/R3/R14/R22; governance never authorizes settlement or changes agent tools.

Tier 1 is synchronous, sequential and process-local. Its execution UUID provides correlation, not durable deduplication. Before enabling inference across human-review resume, restart or multiple workers, provide shared durable conservative admission that preserves spend, request count and uncertain/halted state; never give an invoice a fresh allowance by reopening the context. No live provider adapter, inference agents or control-plane deployment is supplied. Tests use the real TokenOps governor with fake dispatch responses; they do not establish model accuracy or production restart enforcement.

Chronicle body capture, recording and export are disabled within the invoice boundary. Importing app.agents disables implicit python-dotenv loading before TokenOps imports; app.config remains the settings owner. Keep numeric usage/cost logs free of document text. Claude AI guidance and complete Codex mirrors carry the adoption rule; regenerate mirrors after future Claude source changes. See docs/TOKENOPS.md for dispatch pricing-mode restrictions, pre-call bounds and evidence.
