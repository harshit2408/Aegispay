P0-15 independent verification on 2026-10-26 passed on native Postgres18.6 and Python3.14.0 with pinned LangGraph1.2.16/checkpoint4.3.0/checkpoint-postgres3.2.0. Two hard exits followed by a third fresh process preserve the acceptance receipt; exact effects remain prepare1, review-before2, review-after1, finish1. See docs/spikes/SPIKE-9.md and PRD18.

PRD8/11.3 resolves approval consumption-before-validation: serialized dispatch binds an immutable approval to the server-read pending checkpoint/interrupt, commits it unconsumed, invokes, reads a durable receipt, then marks consumed. App transactions and saver are not atomic; recovery must inspect lineage and fail closed on ambiguity. Consumed votes remain eligible for gate counting unless voided or stale. This is the future product contract, not an implemented auth/router/recovery system. Concurrency and all app/saver crash gaps remain P1/P2 tests. No PayPal or secret-file access in this closeout.
