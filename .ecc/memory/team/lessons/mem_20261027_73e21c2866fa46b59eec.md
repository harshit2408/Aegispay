2026-10-27, Dev B. Follow-up to the halt failures recorded in the linked lesson. Acquiring an OAuth token may itself perform HTTP and allow the authoritative halt or trusted-record state to change. A precondition check made before token acquisition is therefore too early, even for an initial payout attempt. The PayPal client now invokes a caller-supplied before_send callback after token acquisition and immediately before HTTP dispatch. Settlement supplies its final R20 precondition check at that boundary.

Settlement owns payout retries. retry=False permits one payout dispatch per service attempt; a 401 cannot silently produce a second payout POST below the service's precondition checks. A subsequent service attempt rechecks the frozen command, halt and trusted-record versions after refreshed credentials are available. Preserve both initial-token-acquisition and 401-refresh halt regressions when changing client retries. Sources: backend/app/paypal/client.py, backend/app/paypal/payouts.py, backend/app/settlement/service.py and their tests. Final combined-suite evidence belongs to the batch entry in docs/SESSION_LOG.md; this memory does not assert the pending final rerun or a live sandbox result.

The earlier review reproductions remain accurate evidence about their named revisions. This correction supersedes that known dispatch gap on the batch branch; it does not rewrite historical review findings or claim independent developer approval. No real PayPal/LLM request or credential read was needed for the regressions.
